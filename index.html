  • Gamepad — stick/D-pad fly · A fire/confirm · X/RB rocket · B/LB bomb · Start pause

Take off vertically, cross the sea, bomb the coast, return left to the carrier and descend gently onto the deck.

Inspired by Harrier Attack! (Durell Software, 1983) — original design by Ron Jeffs and Robert White; Spectrum version by Mike A. Richardson. All code, art and audio here are original. The screenshots in screenshots/ are private reference material and are not shipped. Single-file edition of engine v0.9: finite-weapon aimed test flight and varied, stable scenery.
